Sunrise at Osmeña Peak

Posted on September 12, 2026

Osmeña Peak in Dalaguete is the highest point in Cebu. The trail to the top takes about 20 minutes from the jump-off point, so it is a good first hike for beginners.

At the top you get a view of jagged green hills that look like a smaller version of the Chocolate Hills.

Tip: Start walking before 5 AM if you want to catch the sunrise.

Swimming at Kawasan Falls

Posted on September 26, 2026

Kawasan Falls in Badian is known for its bright blue water. You can rent a bamboo raft and float right under the main falls.

Many visitors combine the trip with canyoneering, which ends at the falls after a few hours of jumping and swimming downstream.

Tip: Bring water shoes. The rocks near the falls are slippery.

Snorkeling in Moalboal

Posted on October 3, 2026

Moalboal is famous for its sardine run, a huge school of sardines that stays close to the shore at Panagsama Beach.

You only need a snorkel and a mask to see them. If you are lucky, a sea turtle might swim by too.

Tip: Bring your own snorkel to save on rental fees.
